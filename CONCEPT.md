## Visual direction
- **Palette:** Forest sage (`#1f5c4a`) + soft mint (`#a8d5c4`) on cool ivory — calm clinical greens, deliberately softer and greener than Bright Smile’s deep dental teal, and nowhere near Shivam’s navy/gold.
- **Typography:** Fraunces (headings) + Manrope (UI/body) — warm clinical editorial, not dental Cormorant or property Playfair.
- **Photography:** Professional manual therapy, active rehab, mobility & strength (Unsplash) — no cartoons, no toy icons, no stock “fake doctor” portraits of the owners.
- **Motion:** Soft scroll reveals, subtle ken-burns on hero; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Differentiating layout idea
**“Path back to movement”** — hero manual-therapy close-up with New Panvel East eyebrow → trust rail (10+ years · ISO · dual PT specialists · hours) → photo service cards (Neuro · Spine · Sports · Manual) → doctor credentials strip (text-led, no invented faces) → dual CTA banner → map + sticky Call/WhatsApp. Replaces their live site’s placeholder 0+ stats and weak CTAs.
